On Saturday, December 13, StuyPulse will be hosting our annual Stuy Splash event at [Stuyvesant High School](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Stuyvesant+High+School). There will be lectures by team members and guest speakers on a variety of topics, including CAD, strategy, software development practices, and marketing. Registration starts at 9:15am, and the event lasts until 3pm. Please feel free to join us!
